# Drum Studio "Rock" kit samples

From the drum samples that ship with **Sonic Pi** (https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi, `etc/samples/`), which
are originally from freesound.org and placed in the **public domain under CC0 1.0** (see SONIC-PI-SAMPLES-README.md,
copied from Sonic Pi). All but one are from the same acoustic kit, recorded by *menegass* on freesound.org.

Downloaded on 2026-10-15. Each was trimmed at the start, cut to length with a fade-out, loudness-matched and
converted to mono 96 kbps MP3 with ffmpeg.
